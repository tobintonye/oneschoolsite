What OneSchool does

Four jobs every school has to do, every term, handled in one system.

Results without the spreadsheets

Teachers enter each student's scores for their subjects. OneSchool does the calculations and gives the school report cards it can download and print.

  • GPA and class positions worked out automatically
  • A report card for every student
  • CBT scores can go straight into the results, so nothing is typed twice

Fees without the queue

Parents pay school fees online, from a phone or a computer, through secure payment gateways. Each payment goes onto the student's account.

  • The school sees what has been paid and what is still owed
  • One fee account for every student
  • Guardian phone numbers and emails kept for billing notices

Exams on school computers

Set computer-based tests and let students take them in the computer lab. The scores go into the result sheets, so nobody has to mark and re-enter them.

  • Scores flow straight into results
  • Less marking and re-typing for teachers

Student records and attendance

Every student has one record: bio-data, enrollment details, academic history and attendance. It works for primary and secondary schools and for higher education.

  • Attendance recorded once and used in every report
  • Each teacher has their own login and profile
  • Records are encrypted with AES-256 and belong to the school

One term, start to finish

This is how a normal term runs when the school uses OneSchool.

  1. 1Before term starts

    Classes, subjects and student records are set up. New students are enrolled and guardian contact details are updated.

  2. 2Through the term

    Attendance is recorded. Parents pay fees online, and each payment is recorded on the student's account.

  3. 3Exam time

    Exams can be taken on school computers. The scores go into the result sheets.

  4. 4End of term

    Teachers enter their scores. OneSchool works out GPA and positions, and the report cards are ready to download and print.

  5. 5Next term

    Each student's academic history stays on their record, and the school can see any balances still owed.

Questions schools ask

Which schools can use OneSchool?+

OneSchool is built for primary and secondary schools as well as higher education institutions.

What do teachers have to do each term?+

Enter the scores for their subjects, or set CBT tests. OneSchool works out GPA and class positions and prepares the report cards.

How do parents pay fees?+

Parents pay online through secure payment gateways. Each payment is recorded against the student account.

Can we run exams on computers?+

Yes. OneSchool includes computer-based tests, and the scores feed directly into result processing.

Where is our data kept and who can see it?+

Student, guardian and staff records are encrypted with AES-256. We do not share, sell or trade them. Data is only disclosed when the law requires it, or to provide a service your school has asked for, such as payment processing.

Who owns the school data, and what if we stop using OneSchool?+

The school owns its data. If you leave, institutional data is returned to the school or permanently deleted after 60 days, unless the law requires otherwise.

Who do we contact about privacy questions?+

Write to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@oneschool.ng.
