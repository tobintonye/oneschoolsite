What schools gain from OneSchool

Less manual work in every term, and one reliable source for results, fees and attendance.

  • Faster result processing

    Calculations and positions are done by the system, so there is less manual work and fewer corrections.

  • Clear fee records

    Online payments land on the student account, so the school can see what is paid and what is owed.

  • Consistent attendance data

    One attendance record feeds every report instead of separate registers.

  • Less paperwork for staff

    Records, scores and exams live in one place, so teachers spend more time teaching.

How it works

The two processes schools ask about most, from start to finish.

From scores to report cards

  1. 1
    Enter scores

    Teachers record scores, or CBT results are imported automatically.

  2. 2
    Results are calculated

    OneSchool works out GPA and class positions for every student.

  3. 3
    Report cards are ready

    Report cards are generated and can be downloaded and printed.

From payment to balance

  1. 1
    Parent pays online

    Fees are paid through a secure payment gateway, from any phone or computer.

  2. 2
    Payment is recorded

    The payment is applied to the student account straight away.

  3. 3
    School sees the balance

    Administrators can see what is paid and what is still owed.

Frequently asked questions

Which schools can use OneSchool?+

OneSchool is built for primary and secondary schools as well as higher education institutions.

How do parents pay fees?+

Parents pay online through secure payment gateways. Each payment is recorded against the student account.

Can we run exams on computers?+

Yes. OneSchool includes computer-based tests, and the scores feed directly into result processing.

Who owns the school data?+

The school does. OneSchool acts as a data processor on the school's behalf.

What happens to our data if we stop using OneSchool?+

Institutional data is returned to the school or permanently deleted after 60 days, unless the law requires otherwise.

Who do we contact about privacy questions?+

Write to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@oneschool.ng.
